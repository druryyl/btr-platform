# BUSINESS PROPOSAL CREATION SKILL

## PURPOSE

Create persuasive B2B business proposals that sell **operational improvement**, not software.

The objective is to help business decision makers understand operational problems, recognize their business impact, trust the proposed solution, and approve the project.

This skill is intended for software consulting proposals where the software is merely a tool to improve the customer's daily operation.

---

# CORE PHILOSOPHY

Every proposal MUST consistently communicate the following philosophy.

> **Operasional yang lebih ringan. Dibangun dari cara kerja yang nyata.**

Software should always follow how people actually work.

Never redesign operational processes merely to satisfy software.

The proposal must communicate practical operational thinking instead of software capability.

---

# BRAND POSITIONING

The proposal should position the solution as:

- operational improvement
- practical implementation
- realistic adoption
- measurable operational change

Never position the proposal as:

- software sales
- technology showcase
- digital transformation campaign

The software is an enabler.

Operational improvement is the product.

---

# TAGLINE

Default proposal tagline:

> **Pragmatis. Realistis. Berorientasi Hasil.**

---

# LANGUAGE

Unless explicitly requested otherwise,

**ALL proposals MUST be written in Bahasa Indonesia.**

Use natural Indonesian business language.

Avoid translated-English wording.

Prefer terminology already familiar to Owners, Directors, Operational Managers, Warehouse Managers and Supervisors.

The proposal should feel like it was written by an experienced Indonesian business consultant, not by an AI translator.

---

# TARGET AUDIENCE

Primary audience:

- Business Owner
- Director
- General Manager

Secondary audience:

- Operational Manager
- Department Manager
- Warehouse Manager
- Logistics Manager

Assume the readers are experienced business people.

Do NOT assume technical knowledge.

Avoid software engineering terminology.

---

# WRITING STYLE

The writing should be:

- professional
- calm
- confident
- practical
- realistic
- concise

Avoid:

- academic writing
- software documentation
- marketing brochure style
- startup language

Short paragraphs are preferred.

Every page should communicate one key message.

---

# WORDING PRINCIPLES

The proposal MUST use **realistic and pragmatic wording**.

Every statement should describe something that can be:

- observed
- measured
- verified
- experienced directly
- recognized in daily operation

Avoid subjective statements.

Examples to avoid:

- pelaporan menjadi lebih baik
- proses menjadi lebih optimal
- meningkatkan efisiensi
- meningkatkan produktivitas
- meningkatkan visibilitas
- monitoring lebih mudah
- meningkatkan kualitas operasional

Those statements may only appear when immediately followed by a concrete explanation.

Always explain:

HOW.

WHAT changes.

WHO experiences it.

WHAT becomes observable.

Example:

Bad

> Pelaporan menjadi lebih baik.

Better

> Dashboard menampilkan KPI Outstanding Shipment, Shipment Completed, dan Shipment Failed tanpa harus merekap laporan driver secara manual.

Bad

> Monitoring lebih mudah.

Better

> Supervisor dapat melihat Shipment Planned, Ready, Departed, In Delivery, dan Completed pada hari yang sama.

Bad

> Operasional lebih efisien.

Better

> Petugas tidak lagi mencatat hasil stok di kertas kemudian mengetiknya kembali ke komputer.

---

# SHOW, DON'T CLAIM

Never persuade readers using marketing claims.

Persuade them using operational facts.

Do not write:

> Sistem meningkatkan efisiensi.

Instead write:

> Planner tidak lagi menyusun pengiriman melalui percakapan WhatsApp. Setiap Shipment memiliki kendaraan, driver, tanggal, dan urutan pelanggan yang tercatat dalam satu rencana kerja.

Do not write:

> Sistem meningkatkan kontrol.

Instead write:

> Supervisor meninjau selisih stok sebelum stok diperbarui sehingga seluruh koreksi memiliki jejak pemeriksaan.

Operational change is always more persuasive than abstract benefits.

---

# ORIGINALITY

Every proposal should feel original.

Never generate generic consulting language.

Avoid common software proposal wording such as:

- transformasi digital
- digital ecosystem
- smart solution
- innovative platform
- one stop solution
- future ready
- next generation
- end-to-end platform
- meningkatkan efisiensi bisnis
- solusi digital terintegrasi

Originality must come from:

- real operational observations
- customer terminology
- practical workflow
- concrete examples
- measurable operational changes

Originality should never rely on unusual vocabulary.

It should come from practical business thinking.

---

# BUSINESS FIRST

Never explain software first.

Always explain:

Current operational work

↓

Operational problem

↓

Business consequence

↓

Operational improvement

↓

Software capability

Software is always the final explanation.

Never the first.

---

# PROPOSAL STORYTELLING

The proposal should read like a business story.

Current Situation

↓

Operational Problems

↓

Business Consequences

↓

Operational Improvement

↓

Solution

↓

Implementation

↓

Investment

↓

Decision

Readers should naturally arrive at the conclusion:

"This is the right investment."

---

# QUICK ROI THINKING

Every proposed module should answer:

"What repetitive work disappears?"

"What coordination becomes unnecessary?"

"What manual activity becomes shorter?"

"What operational uncertainty becomes visible?"

Never simply say:

"Increase productivity."

Always explain the operational mechanism.

---

# PROPOSAL STRUCTURE

Recommended flow:

1 Cover

2 Executive Summary

3 Why Now? (optional but recommended)

4 Current Operational Challenges

5 Business Impact

6 Solution Overview

7 Philosophy of the Solution (recommended)

8-12 Product Modules

13 Integrated Business Value

14 Implementation Approach

15 Investment

16 Closing

The number of pages may vary depending on the proposal.

---

# PHILOSOPHY PAGE

Whenever appropriate, include one page explaining the philosophy behind the solution.

Recommended principles:

---

## Sistem mengikuti pekerjaan.

Bukan sebaliknya.

---

## Perubahan dilakukan bertahap.

Mulai dari aktivitas yang paling sering dikerjakan.

---

## Keberhasilan diukur dari pekerjaan yang menjadi lebih ringan.

Bukan dari banyaknya fitur.

---

This page should explain how the consultant thinks.

Not how the software works.

---

# SOFTWARE POSITIONING

Never present software as the final product.

The software should always be introduced as:

- alat kerja
- pendukung operasional
- media pelaksanaan proses

Never imply that software itself creates business value.

Business value comes from operational improvement.

---

# IMPLEMENTATION

Always recommend phased implementation.

Preferred sequence:

Discovery

↓

Configuration

↓

Pilot

↓

Evaluation

↓

Rollout

↓

Support

Recommend small successful pilots instead of big-bang implementation.

# CUSTOMER LANGUAGE

Whenever possible,

replace software terminology with customer terminology.

Example:

Instead of

"Entity"

write

"Pelanggan"

Instead of

"Workflow Engine"

write

"Alur Kerja"

Always speak using the customer's operational language.

---

# CONSULTING MINDSET

Write like an experienced consultant.

Not like a salesperson.

Not like a programmer.

Not like an academic researcher.

The proposal should make readers feel:

"They understand how our operation actually works."

---

# CLOSING

The proposal should reinforce practical business value.

Preferred closing message:

> **Perbaikan operasional tidak selalu dimulai dengan sistem yang lebih besar. Sering kali dimulai dari satu proses yang dibuat lebih sederhana, lebih jelas, dan lebih mudah dijalankan setiap hari.**

Encourage a pilot project.

Do not pressure the customer.

---

# NEVER

Never oversell.

Never promise unrealistic ROI.

Never invent software capabilities.

Never use AI-generated marketing jargon.

Never exaggerate technology.

Never write software documentation.

Never use feature lists as the main selling point.

Never use abstract claims without explaining the operational mechanism.

Never sacrifice realism for persuasion.

---

# SUCCESS CRITERIA

A proposal is considered successful when readers conclude:

✓ Solusi ini realistis.

✓ Solusi ini memahami cara kerja kami.

✓ Implementasinya terlihat aman.

✓ Pengguna lapangan akan mampu menggunakannya.

✓ Dampaknya terhadap pekerjaan sehari-hari terlihat jelas.

✓ Ini bukan proposal software biasa.

✓ Ini adalah proposal perbaikan operasional.

If readers only remember the software features,

the proposal has failed.

If readers remember how their daily work becomes lighter,

the proposal has succeeded.
